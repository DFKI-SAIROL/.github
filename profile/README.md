# Systems AI for Robot Learning (SAIROL)

The [**SAIROL**](https://www.dfki.de/en/web/research/research-departments/systems-ai-for-robot-learning) department at the [German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI)](https://www.dfki.de/en/) develops intelligent robotic systems that learn from interaction with people and their environment.

Our research brings together artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, and system engineering to advance robust, adaptive, and autonomous robot behavior. 
We build and evaluate systems across the full pipeline, from perception and learning to control and real-world deployment.
